A flexible work cell based on a large, high-payload anthropomorphic arm, able to perform circular or linear steel-to-steel welds with a high-power infrared laser and position control through a vision system.

**Main features**

- Installation on a 6-axis robot with a maximum reach of 3.5 metres and a wrist payload of 125 kg
- Solid-state laser source of over 8 kW optical, with power transmitted over armoured optical fibre cooled at the connectors
- Collimation and focusing optics with copper mirrors cooled by circulating deionised water, a removable protective final glass, and crossed compressed-air jets at the exit to deflect splashes of molten metal.
- Automatic vision system able to guide the alignment of the robot-mounted tool on the welding points with a precision of \< 0.02 mm, and a system for measuring the distance of the workpiece using orthogonal laser lines, with a precision of \< 0.1 mm
- Surveillance system for quality control and safety based on 4 IP cameras connected to a recording system
- Complete management of recipe revisions according to quality standards.
- Detailed records of the welding process, with initial and final photos, are kept for every single weld for quality control purposes
- SMS alarm system to report anomalies to the operator on duty during unsupervised automatic operation
