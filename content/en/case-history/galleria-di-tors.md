- Safety systems compliant with European (2004/54/EC) and Italian (Legislative Decree 264 of 5 October 2006) standards, including:
- 8 ventilation accelerators of 25 kW each
- 8 internal and external variable message panels
- Redundant fire detection system with heat-sensitive cable and optical fibre (OTDR with 1-metre resolution)
- Vehicle counting and classification in both directions, entering and leaving (calculation of vehicles present in the tunnel)
- 3 air control stations (pollution and opacity)
- Transit and reinforcement lighting system regulated according to external lighting and veiling luminance (to UNI 11095:2003 standards)
- Internal ultrasonic anemometer
- External weather station
- Double control cabin for the two carriageways, motorisation of all protection switches, double 450 kW generator set, 8 static uninterruptible power supplies for a total of 320 kVA.
