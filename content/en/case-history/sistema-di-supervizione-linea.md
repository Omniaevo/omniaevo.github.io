Main features

- Communication infrastructure based on TCP/IP and RS232 serial protocols
- Integration of machines driven by independent numerical controls
- Identification of workpieces by a barcode printed at the start of the line and tracked through the processing steps
- Display of partial progress data on two peripheral panel PCs
- Reception of the production list from the company management system (ERP SAP R/3)
- Real-time update of the automatic production progress status on the company management system
